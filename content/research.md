My current research lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Computer Vision**, and **Cognitive Science**. I am broadly interested in leveraging advanced machine learning techniques to model human perception and behavior, aiming to bridge the gap between artificial intelligence and human cognition.

---

### Core Research Themes

#### 1. Human Visual Attention & Scanpath Prediction
How do humans perceive their environments, and how does their attention shift when presented with visual stimuli? I am particularly focused on **Scanpath Prediction** (predicting the sequence of eye fixations). Modeling these scanpaths helps us understand the underlying cognitive and behavioral mechanisms of human vision.

Our approach integrates:
* **Deep Learning & Computer Vision**: Using deep neural networks to extract spatial, semantic, and temporal features from complex visual scenes.
* **Depth-Aware Vision**: Humans can effortlessly perceive 3D structures and depth even when viewing 2D images. I believe that integrating **depth information** (3D layout, distance cues) is crucial to improving scanpath predictions and modeling realistic human attention.

#### 2. Understanding Cognitive and Behavioral Mechanisms via AI
My ultimate goal is to explore how AI models can serve as computational tools to help us achieve a better understanding of human cognitive and behavioral mechanisms. Even if these deep learning models function to some extent as a "black box," they can still provide valuable predictive power and insights into how humans process complex visual information and make decisions in real-world environments.
